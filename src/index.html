Tasks

📁

          

            
Projects

            
Group related tasks. Create one from the sidebar with the small +.

          

        

        

          
🤝

          

            
Collaboration

            
Open a project and invite someone by email. They'll get it in their notification bell.

          

        

        

          
📅

          

            
Calendar & Notes

            
Keep dates and quick thoughts alongside your tasks. They sync to your account.
