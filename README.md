# Aschertype

A privacy-first productivity suite. Tasks, projects, calendar, notes, and focus sessions in one app. Runs as a web app, an installable PWA, and a desktop application.

**[Open the app →](https://aschertype.vercel.app)**

---

## What it does

Aschertype keeps everything in one place instead of spread across four different tools.

- **Tasks** with priorities and due dates, grouped into projects
- **Projects** you can share with other people by email
- **Calendar** for events and reminders
- **Notes** organized by category
- **Pomodoro** timer for focus sessions
- **Daily closeout** to review unfinished work before you sign off

## Getting started

1. Open [aschertype.vercel.app](https://aschertype.vercel.app)
2. Create an account with your email, or press **Continue without an account** to try it out
3. Add your first task in the box at the top

The first time you sign in you'll get a short tour that explains the rest.

## Using it without an account

Guest mode stores everything on your device only. It works fully offline. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, the data doesn't follow you. Sign up later and future changes sync everywhere.

## Sharing a project

1. Create a project from the sidebar
2. Open it and click **Invite**
3. Enter the email of the person you want to share with
4. Choose what they can do (add tasks, rename tasks, remove tasks, rename the project)
5. They'll get an invite in their notification bell

By default a collaborator can view tasks and check them off. Everything else is off until you turn it on.

## Installing it

Aschertype is a Progressive Web App. On Chrome, Edge, and Safari, look for the install button in the address bar or the Share menu. Once installed it launches like a normal app and works offline.

## Notifications

Turn on browser notifications in **Settings → Notifications** to get alerts for upcoming calendar events and when a Pomodoro session is about to end.

## Themes

Light and dark modes are both available in **Settings → Theme**. By default the app follows your system setting.

## If something isn't working

- **Didn't get a confirmation email?** Check your spam folder. First-time senders often land there.
- **Forgot your password?** Use the "Forgot password?" link on the sign-in screen.
- **Data not syncing across devices?** Make sure you're signed in with the same account on both, and that both devices are online.
- **Something else?** Open an issue on this repository.
